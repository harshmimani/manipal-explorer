# Festivals research notes (28 Nov 2026 – 31 Jan 2027)

Researched 24 Nov 2026. Data: `festivals.json` (29 entries). Panchang dates use Drik Panchang set to Mangaluru (geoname-id 1263780).

## Confirmed from published schedules
- **Kukke Champa Shashti**: jatre 5–21 Dec 2026. Panchami rathotsava on the night of 14 Dec, **Maharathotsava early morning on 15 Dec**, Avabhritha on 16 Dec, Laksha Deepotsava on 8 Dec, flower chariot on 13 Dec, Kiru Shashti on 14 Jan 2027. Source: the temple's announcement as reported by V4 News on 22 Nov 2026. Drik Panchang (Mangaluru) agrees on 15 Dec. US-located panchang pages show 14 Dec; ignore those.
- **Dharmasthala Laksha Deepotsava**: **4–8 Dec 2026**, so it falls inside the window (Karthika Amavasya is 8 Dec 2026). The full programme is in V4 News, 23 Nov 2026. Samavasarana puja is on 9 Dec. Not yet cross-checked against shridharmasthala.org, whose events API returned nothing for the period.
- **Kambala**: uses the State Kambala Association's **revised** calendar (V4 News, 12 Nov 2026). It differs from the July 2026 Deccan Herald list for several venues: Venur moved to 19 Dec (was Mar 20), Mangaluru to 2 Jan (was 19 Dec), Miyar to 9 Jan (was 2 Jan), Naringana to 16 Jan (was 9 Jan), Puttur to 30 Jan (was 6 Feb), and Aikala to 6 Feb (was 30 Jan, so now outside the window). Adve, Jeppinamogaru, Bangadi and Venur's old March slot are all outside the window. End dates are null because races run overnight under the 24/30-hour rule.
- **Yakshagana**: the 2026–27 tour start dates come from Hosakannada, 22 Nov 2026. About 45 melas are touring. Season end (Pattanaje, around late May) is based on the traditional pattern, not a 2027 announcement.

## Panchang-derived (not temple-announced)
- Vaikuntha Ekadashi / Gita Jayanti on 20 Dec 2026. Some Smarta and Vaishnava listings differ by a day.
- Hanuman Jayanti (Karnataka, Margashira Shukla Trayodashi) on 22 Dec 2026.
- Datta Jayanti on 23 Dec 2026.
- Makara Sankranti on **15 Jan 2027**. The sankramana is late on 14 Jan, and some calendars list 14 Jan.

## Unconfirmed (null dates, "check locally")
- **Udupi Saptotsava 2027**: not announced. If the Matha observes Sankranti on 15 Jan, it would run about 10–16 Jan. That is an inference and is not in the data.
- **Kadri Manjunatha jatre 2027**: not announced. The pattern is about 10 days from Sankranti; in 2026 it ran 14–24 Jan with the rathotsava on 21 Jan.
- **Saligrama Guru Narasimha car festival**: Makara masa, which Wikipedia gives as 16 Jan. No 2027 announcement found.
- **Koteshwara Kodi Habba**: no 2026 date found. By the Vrischika-full-moon pattern (27 Nov 2023, 15 Dec 2024, 4–5 Dec 2025), 2026 may fall around 24 Nov, which is **before the window**. Phone the temple.
- **Karavali Utsav Mangaluru**: "December" planned (2 Nov 2026), but dates and venues are not announced.
- **Karavali Utsav Karwar**: no 2026 information found.
- Dress codes, darshan timings and annadana/prasada meal times for Kukke, Dharmasthala and Udupi during the festivals were not verified, because the search budget ran out. They are flagged "verify locally" in the tips. Fill them in from the temples research file.

## Outside the window
- **Udupi Paryaya**: Shiroor Matha (Sri Vedavardhana Teertha) took charge on 18 Jan 2026 for 2026–28. The next changeover is on **18 Jan 2028**, to Sode Matha (Wikipedia; the swamiji is not named). There is no Paryaya in Jan 2027.
- **Sirsi Marikamba Jatre**: biennial. The 2026 edition happened; the next is listed for 2028, and it is usually in Feb–Mar. Not in the window.
- **Kollur, Kateel, Mandarthi, Mangaladevi, Gokarna**: my searches found no major jatre or rathotsava for these temples between 28 Nov and 31 Jan. Apart from the Kateel melas starting on 3 Dec, nothing was added. This was not exhaustively verified.
- **Sringeri / Kukke Makara Sankranti**: no special Sankranti event was found apart from Kukke's Kiru Shashti on 14 Jan. Sringeri was not researched (search budget).
- **Christmas**: 25 Dec is fixed. 2026 mass timings for Rosario Cathedral and Milagres were not published at research time.
