## Places added beyond the list, and why
- **Venur Bahubali** (heritage): a 38 ft Bahubali monolith from 1604, raised by Timmanna Ajila on the Phalguni (Wikipedia). At 70 km it fits easily into a Gadaikallu or Dharmasthala ride, and it's far less crowded than Dharmasthala's.
- **Shishila Shishileshwara / Matsya Theertha** (gods): a Shiva temple on the Kapila river with sacred fish, a fishing ban since 1930, and a memorial to the 1996 poisoning (Wikipedia). It's a distinctive stop near Kokkada and Sowthadka.
- **Split-out entries:** Ratnagiri Bahubali + Manjusha + Vintage Car Museum is its own heritage entry, with official opening hours. Talacauvery–Bhagamandala is a separate gods entry from the Coorg long ride.
- **Not added:** Surya Sadashiva Rudra temple and a Kuduremukh-side drive. The search budget ran out before I could source either properly.

## Closures, bans and season issues
- **Kumara Parvatha (critical):** trekking resumed 18 Oct 2026 only on the Beedahalli–KP–Beedahalli route (Daijiworld, 17 Oct 2026). No date had been set for the Kukke Subrahmanya route (Daijiworld; Elneeru updated 31 Oct 2026). Other rules:
  - Booking is online only, on Aranya Vihaara.
  - Overnight stays and camping are banned, including at Bhattara Mane/Girigadde (NTCA-linked, Mar 2026 reports).
  - The Kukke cap was set at 150/day (Mar 2026). The portal showed Beedahalli at 300 slots/day in Nov 2026.
  - Closed every monsoon and summer.
- **Aranya Vihaara portal rules from 15 Jul 2026:** book 1–30 days ahead. Cancel 8+ days out for a 50% refund; within 7 days, nothing back. Carry the original ID used to book.
- **Bandaje:** the "Bandaje Falls" and "Ballalarayana Durga" treks were open as of Nov 2026, both from the Chikkamagaluru/Durgadahalli side. The "Bandaje via Volambra" route (Belthangady side) was listed closed. The falls thin to a trickle by Dec–Feb.
- **Gadaikallu:** entry only Oct–May (Karnataka Tourism), 6 am–4 pm, no overnight stays.
- **Ermai:** a 2021 report says the private landowner closed the path to the main falls, and Trip.com lists it as closed. A fatal fall happened here in May 2018. Verdict: skip.
- **Charmadi Ghat:** no Nov 2026 ban was found. Past bans:
  - heavy vehicles banned in 2018
  - night ban in 2021, with light vehicles allowed only 6 am–7 pm
  - landslide at the 10th turn in Jul 2024
  - Shiradi closure diverting traffic onto Charmadi in Jun 2025
  - one source (now a 410 page) mentioned a closure until 11 Aug 2026, which couldn't be verified
  It's daylight-only advice anyway.
- **Kukke:** heavy crowds on Nov–Dec festival days.
- **Dharmasthala:** darshan, meals and rooms may vary on crowded days (official notice).
- **Research limit:** I hit the web-search budget partway through. Later items (Charmadi current status, Coorg/Belur temple details, Surya temple) used direct page fetches only.

## Must-do nominations for Dec–Jan
1. **Dharmasthala + Ratnagiri Bahubali + Sowthadka**: one easy day ride. It has an official, verified darshan and annadana schedule, and clear winter views from Ratnagiri.
2. **Charmadi Ghat to Belur–Halebidu (overnight)**: dry ghat roads are at their best, and the UNESCO 2023 Hoysala temples make the strongest long ride of the season.
3. **Gadaikallu (Jamalabad Fort)**: it's open in exactly this window (Oct–May), 88 km away, and dramatic. Start at the gate's opening time.
