## How the list was split or merged
- **Kemmannu hanging bridge & Hoode** and **Swarna River kayaking** are two entries. The paddling (operators, prices, mangroves) is a different outing from the bridge, beach and sunset stop. Both use the Kemmannu coordinates because that's where the Swarna kayak put-in is.
- **Anantheshwara & Chandramouleeshwara** are one entry. They sit on the same square and are visited together, before Krishna.
- **Karkala Bahubali + Chaturmukha Basadi** are one entry (a single day trip).
- **Kapu Marigudi** is folded into the Kaup entry. Wikipedia only names the Hale and Hosa Marigudi shrines, and no source gave enough for a standalone entry.
- **Brahmavar** gets an honest entry: decJanVerdict "ok". It's a highway junction town, and no source names a headline sight in it. Its value is Barkur, Mandarthi, Saligrama and the Hangarakatte ferry.

## Places added beyond the list
1. **Kunjarugiri Durga Temple (Durga Betta).** A hilltop Durga temple credited to Parashurama, with four ponds and a rock-cave footprint shrine to Madhvacharya. It's 1–2 km from Pajaka, so it pairs naturally with the Pajaka entry. Sourced from Wikipedia and Native Planet.
2. **Barkur.** The old Alupa capital, 3 km from Brahmavar: Kattale Basadi ruins with a 20-ft monolithic pillar, Panchalingeshwara, Chowlikere Ganapathy and the Vijayanagara fort ruins. This is what actually makes the Brahmavar area worth a ride. Sourced from Wikipedia.

## Closures, bans, season issues
- **St Mary's Island / Malpe water sports:** a ~4-month monsoon ban (mid-May to mid-Sept) is imposed every year. For 2026 it was lifted on 18 Sept 2026 (News Karnataka, 20 Sept 2026), so it's open for the whole Dec–Jan window. No overnight stays. Don't climb the basalt columns (protected National Geological Monument). Swim only in designated zones.
- **Malpe:** don't enter the water from the Sea Walk (KT). The beach is barricaded in the monsoon.
- **Delta Beach / Kodi Bengre:** strong river-mouth currents, so don't swim there.
- **Mattu:** lifeguards may not be present.
- **Kaup:** the river mouth is dangerous at high tide, and the rocks are slippery.
- **Krishna Matha dress code (new):** since 19 Jan 2026, men must be shirtless all day inside the Matha premises, not just until Mahapuja.
- NH66 (Kaup, Padubidri, Saligrama, Brahmavar) has heavy, fast truck and bus traffic, so night riding is flagged.

## Dec–Jan festival hooks in this region
- Makara Sankranti (mid-Jan): festival at the Krishna Matha, on KT's festival list.
- Saligrama Guru Narasimha Ratharohana: 16 Jan per Wikipedia.
- Attur (St. Lawrence) church feast, Karkala: January (Wikipedia). Expect traffic.
- There's no Paryaya in the window; the next is in Jan 2028.

## Must-do nominations for Dec–Jan
1. **St Mary's Island.** A 90-million-year-old basalt geology monument, only reachable Oct–May. Boats are confirmed running for the 2026–27 season, and it pairs with Malpe sunset.
2. **Udupi Sri Krishna Matha + Car Street after dark.** The spiritual and culinary heart of the region: Kanakana Kindi darshan, free prasada bhojana, and the evening rituals. It's 5 km away, so it can be repeated. Mid-January brings Makara Sankranti.
3. **Kemmannu backwaters + Swarna kayaking + Delta Beach loop.** One 20-km ride gives the hanging bridge, a mangrove paddle, and a sunset on the Kodi Bengre spit, all at their best in the dry season.
