## Places added beyond the list (and why)
1. **Sultan Battery (Boloor):** an ASI-protected Tipu Sultan watchtower (Wikipedia). It is also the jetty for the Tannirbhavi ferry, so it fits naturally into the Tannirbhavi trip.
2. **St Aloysius Chapel paintings:** Antonio Moscheni's interior, with about 600 m² of frescoes and 400 m² of ceiling canvases (Wikipedia). It's the best art interior in the region and a good midday stop while temples are closed. Marked verify-locally because hours, fees and photo rules aren't online.
3. **Chandragiri Fort (Kasaragod):** a 17th-century Shivappa Nayaka fort above the Payaswini river mouth (Wikipedia, Holidify). It fits a Kasaragod day with Ananthapura, Madhur and Bekal.
4. **Madhur Madanantheshwara Siddhivinayaka Temple:** known for its three-tiered gajaprishta roof and Moodappam seva (Wikipedia), and about 7 km from Kasaragod. It pairs with Ananthapura.

Also: the requested "Mangaluru city by train/bike" became its own entry (`mangaluru-city-by-train-bike`). It covers Central (MAQ, Hampankatta, terminus) vs Junction (MAJN, Padil, ex-Kankanadi, about 6 km apart).

Not added: Kulai/Surathkal lighthouse (coordinates found, but no visitor/access info sourced) and Nileshwar backwaters (no sources opened before the search limit).

## Closures, bans, season issues
- **Monsoon beach rules (June 2026):** Panambur, Tannirbhavi and Someshwara were on high alert. All water sports, boating and floating-bridge access were suspended until the end of the monsoon. These rules recur each year and should be over by Dec, but check for fresh notices.
- **Drownings:** Sasihithlu Mooda (Aug 2025, 1 dead) and Someshwara Rudrapade rocks (Sept 2023, doctor slipped). Swimming is not advised at Someshwara at any time.
- **Panambur:** walking on the port breakwater is forbidden.
- **Pilikula:** closed Mondays.
- **Bekal Fort:** open 8:00–5:30 daily (Incredible India). ₹25 counter ticket for Indians, free under 15, ₹300 for foreigners, online via ASI (Onmanorama, 13 Oct 2026).
- **Ananthapura crocodile:** Babiya died on 9 Oct 2022. A new, smaller crocodile was first reported 8 Nov 2023 and confirmed by the temple chairman. It was seen and filmed on the bank on about 16 June 2024 and called "Babiya-3" (Kerala Kaumudi, 17 Jun 2024). **I found no reports after mid-2024**, so whether it is still there is unverified; Incredible India says "a single new crocodile has been spotted since late 2023".
- **Kateel Yakshagana:** there have been 7 troupes since 16 Nov 2025. The season runs from November (after Tulasi Pooja) to May (Pattanaje), and shows now end by midnight. Bookings are reportedly full for 15 years, so you watch, you don't book.

## Must-do nominations for Dec–Jan
1. **Kateel temple + a Kateel mela Yakshagana night.** Peak season, 50 km from Manipal, and a temple meal before the show. It's the most "only here" experience in the region.
2. **Bekal Fort, on a Kasaragod day with Ananthapura.** Verified hours and fees, the best weather of the year, and the lake temple's crocodile story makes a good hook.
3. **Sasihithlu surf lesson.** Nov–Apr is surf season, and it is under an hour from Manipal. Go only with a surf school because of the river-mouth currents.
